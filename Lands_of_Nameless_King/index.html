Lands of Nameless King

Game Rules

  • Goal: Divide the land so each house receives a connected property equal to the number on the house (including the house cell).
  • Controls: Left-click or drag to build/erase walls. Right-click to mark/unmark property cells.
  • One house per property: Two houses cannot belong to the same property.
  • Separated properties: Different properties must not touch horizontally or vertically.
  • Wall system: All walls must connect horizontally or vertically into a single network.
  • No solid walls: Walls cannot completely occupy any 2x2 area.
