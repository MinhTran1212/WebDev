# Exam rules

Read the rules below carefully before you start the exam. By accepting them you declare that
you will follow them. The declaration, with your name and Neptun code, becomes part of your
submitted solution.

## Your own work

- You write the solution alone, as your own work.
- During the exam you may not communicate with anyone (in person, by phone, messaging apps,
  e-mail, social media or any other way), use anyone else's solution, or share your own.
- You may not use artificial intelligence tools (e.g. ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, Gemini, AI code
  completion extensions).
- You may only use the materials allowed by the invigilators.

## Devices

- You may only use the lab computer assigned to you; your own phone, laptop, smartwatch and other
  devices must not be in use.

## Submission

- Submit your solution as the ZIP file created in the exam assistant, uploaded to the exam portal
  before the exam time ends.
- Only solutions uploaded before the deadline can be graded.

## Violations

Breaking these rules may invalidate your exam and lead to disciplinary proceedings under the
university's regulations.
